How to Submit Content

Contribute to the NautilusOS community by submitting your content.

Submission Guide

Contact the NautilusOS team to submit your apps, games, or themes.

  1. Prepare Your Content
    Make sure your content follows the community guidelines.
  2. Contact Us
    Reach out to the community managers to submit your content.

Additional Tips

  • Test your content before submitting.
  • Follow the repository's contribution guidelines if available.
  • Be respectful and provide detailed descriptions.
